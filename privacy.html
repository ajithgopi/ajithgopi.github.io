Privacy Policy for Application

Effective Date / Last Updated: August 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ajith Gopi ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") manages user information when you download, install, and use the Application mobile application for iOS and Android devices.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed within Application.

1. Information Collection & Usage

Application is designed with a privacy-first approach:

  • Personal Data: We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address) to external servers.
  • Local Device Storage: Any data created, processed, or saved within Application remains stored strictly on your local device unless you choose to export, back up, or sync it yourself via operating system features (such as iCloud or Google Drive).
  • Usage Data & Crash Reports: The app does not track your personal activity. Standard non-identifiable system diagnostics provided by Apple App Store or Google Play Store (such as anonymous crash logs) may be received if you have opted in to share telemetry with app developers through your device settings.

2. Device Permissions

Depending on the features offered by Application, the app may request specific system permissions on your iOS or Android device:

  • Storage / Media Access: To save or export app files, data backups, or images directly to your device storage.
  • Biometrics (Face ID / Touch ID / Fingerprint): If supported, biometric authentication is processed entirely on-device by the operating system (iOS Secure Enclave or Android Keystore). We do not access or store your biometric data.
  • Network Access: Required only if fetching online updates, exchange rates, or essential remote content. No personal identifiers are transmitted during network calls.
  • Notifications: Used solely to deliver local reminders or app alerts if enabled by you.

You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time through your iOS or Android system settings.

3. Third-Party Services

Application relies primarily on native OS platform tools. If third-party frameworks or distribution services are utilized (such as Google Play Services or Apple App Store framework), their data practices are governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

4. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your data. Since data created within Application is maintained locally on your mobile device, your data benefits from the built-in security features and hardware encryption of iOS and Android operating systems.

5. Children's Privacy

Application does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.

6. Data Deletion & User Rights

Because Application stores data locally on your device, you maintain full control over your data at all times:

  • You can view, edit, or clear your data within the application settings.
  • Uninstalling Application will immediately and permanently delete all app data stored on your device.
  • For any data deletion inquiries or requests, you may contact the developer directly at the email provided below.
