Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I report a bug or issue?

Send an email to me@agopi.in describing the issue. Including your device model, OS version (iOS or Android), and screenshots or steps to reproduce the problem helps us fix it faster.

2. Is my data secure and backed up?

Yes. Data created within Application is stored locally on your device. You can back up or restore your data using standard OS device backup tools (iCloud Backup for iOS or Google One Backup for Android).

3. How do I request a new feature?

We love user feedback! Send us your ideas via email, and we'll evaluate them for future app updates.
